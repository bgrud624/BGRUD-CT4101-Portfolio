Currently, the best model is the random forest one,
after changing the process on selecting the best 
model to consider recall along with precision.
This decision was made because I think false negatives
are more damaging than false positives with smoking,
since the applications of such a model include
sending resources to people who are suspected to be
smokers, in which case sending the resources to those
who don't need it is a fine trade off to ensure
that those who actually do smoke do recieve the 
resources.
The next steps include trying to tune the parameters
of the models to reach 90%+ precision and recall for the
best performing model, which may include trying out new
models or applying new concept from class like neural networkd
with an MLP classifier. This could also involve more
preprocessing to see whether certain omitted features
like nationality make a real difference on the results,
or if removing more features could streamline the models'
accuracy.
